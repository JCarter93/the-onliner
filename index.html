Grade

Build a grade timeline
Strip to video-only tracks Reset Motion to default
Position, scale and rotation. Motion itself cannot be removed, only reset — same for Opacity.
Remove other applied effects Remove disabled clips and hidden tracks
Clips switched off in the edit, and everything on a hidden track, are invisible in the programme but still export into the XML. Gaps are left in place — nothing is rippled, so the conform still lines up.
Remove adjustment layers
They almost always carry the temp grade, which is the last thing the colourist wants arriving on top of their timeline.
Remove clips hidden by layers above
A clip covered for its whole length by a clip on a higher track is invisible in the programme but still conforms from the XML. Turn this off if you stack clips with alpha — a logo or matte covers the time without hiding the picture, and this cannot tell the difference. Partly-covered clips are always kept and listed instead.
Flatten to a single video track
Brings every clip down to V1, the top layer winning — the clip underneath is trimmed back where they overlap. Off by default because it is destructive to real composites: anything stacked for a reason, including alpha graphics, is flattened into the picture or trimmed away. Remove your graphics first.
Auto-export FCPXML Strip empty tracks from the XML
Premiere has no way to delete a track, so the grade sequence keeps the source's full track count — the teaser exported 10 video and 30 audio tracks with clips on one. They come out of the exported file instead. The clip count is checked before and after, and the XML is left untouched if it does not match.
